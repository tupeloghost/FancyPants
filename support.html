Made by a creator, for creators

Fancy Britches is built by one person — Tupelo Ghost, an independent artist — so that music doesn’t have to sit behind a static image ever again. Every world, every mechanic, every line of it was made by hand, for the artists, streamers, and players who show up to play.

It’s free while it grows. If it made you smile, made your song feel bigger, or gave your stream a night to remember — you can help keep the lights on.

First goal: help me pay off my student loans.
Every donation goes to a real person, not a company. When the donation link is live it will appear right here.

Want your music in here?

You can already paste any Suno link and play inside your own song. Bigger artist features — your own analytics, top plays and high scores per song, custom pages — are coming. Join the waiting list on the front page and you’ll be first to know.

Rights & takedowns

Fancy Britches doesn’t host third-party music; pasted songs stream from their source, for play, and are never stored. Paste songs you made or have rights to. If you’re a rights holder and want something removed, email rockypawart@gmail.com and it’ll be handled promptly.
